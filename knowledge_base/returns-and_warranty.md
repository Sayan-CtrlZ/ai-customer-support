# Returns and warranty

## Returns
All orders go through Amazon, so returns do too. The customer starts a return in Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon arranges the pickup and handles the refund.
We cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund from our side. A colleague answers every refund question.

## Warranty
- Mixer grinders: 2 years on the motor base and the motor. 1 year on the jars, lids, gasket, and coupler.
- Bookshelves: 2 years on panels and hardware.
The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover damage from use, such as a burnt motor from running it empty or a cracked jar from a fall.

## Other products
For any other Neem & Teak product, the same rules apply: returns through Amazon within 30 days, and a 2-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
