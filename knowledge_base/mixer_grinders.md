# Mixer grinders

## Sizes
We sell three mixer grinders. Pick the model by family size and how often it is used.
- NT-MG500 (500 W): families of 1 to 2 people. Best for chutneys, small batches, and light daily grinding.
- NT-MG750 (750 W): families of 3 to 5 people. The best all-round model, good for idli and dosa batter and masala.
- NT-MG1000 (1000 W): families of 6 or more, or heavy daily use. Faster, for large batches and hard spices.
A customer between two sizes should take the larger model.

## What is in the box
Every mixer grinder comes with: the motor base, a wet grinding jar, a dry grinding jar, a chutney jar, a lid for each jar, a spare rubber gasket, a spare coupler, and a user manual with the warranty card.
Part numbers: wet jar MG-WET, dry jar MG-DRY, chutney jar MG-CHUT, jar lid MG-LID, gasket MG-GASKET, coupler MG-COUPLER.

## Running time
Run the motor for no more than 2 minutes at a time. Then let it rest for 5 minutes. Fill a jar no more than two thirds full.
A light warm smell on the first few uses is normal. It goes away on its own. That is not a fault.

## Reset check for a motor that stops
Most stops come from the overload protector, not from a motor fault. Ask the customer to do this first:
1. Switch off and unplug the mixer grinder. Wait 20 minutes for the motor to cool.
2. Check that the jar sits fully on the base and the lid is locked in place.
3. Press the red reset button at the back of the base.
4. Plug in and run it on the lowest speed for 30 seconds with the jar half full. Then use it as normal for one day.
If the motor still stops or does not start, ask for the order number, the model, and how many days ago the mixer grinder arrived.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.
- Missing accessory: we ship the missing part. No return needed.
- Motor stops or does not start: reset check first.
- Still faulty after the reset check, within 30 days of delivery: we send a new mixer grinder.
- Still faulty after the reset check, more than 30 days after delivery: we send a service kit.
- Burning smell, smoke, or sparks: always goes to a colleague.
To arrange anything, we need the order number, the model, and a short description of the problem.
